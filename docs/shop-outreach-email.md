# Email to invite shops to list on Cometcart

Send from cometcartonline@gmail.com, one shop at a time (not a mass mailing), to the shop's
general or partnerships email address. Fill in the parts in [square brackets].

---

**Subject:** Free listing for [Shop name] on Cometcart, a South African price comparison site

Hi [name, or "the [Shop name] team"],

I'm Ulemu, and I run **Cometcart** (https://www.cometcart.co.za), a new South African site where shoppers search for products in their own words, compare prices from different shops side by side, and save them to wishlists they share with friends and family.

I'd love to include **[Shop name]**, and it's **free**:

- Your products appear in our Discover section with your price, and every "Visit shop" click goes straight to your product page.
- Prices are always sorted from lowest to highest. Nobody can pay to look cheaper.
- Each month we can send you a report of how many shoppers we sent your way.

**What we'd need from you:** just a reply saying yes, and that we may show your product names, prices, links and product photos on Cometcart.

[If they use Shopify:] Because your store runs on Shopify, we can then add your products and keep prices up to date ourselves. You don't need to do anything else.

[If not:] We'd then ask for a simple spreadsheet of your products (name, category, price, link and photo). Here's the template: https://www.cometcart.co.za/feed-template.csv

Listing is free while we grow. If we ever introduce a small fee per visitor we send you, we'll agree it with you first, and you can leave at any time with no cost. You can also ask us to remove your products at any time by replying to this email.

You can see how it works here: https://www.cometcart.co.za/list-your-shop

Thank you, and I hope to welcome [Shop name] to Cometcart.

Kind regards,
Ulemu Moya
Cometcart, https://www.cometcart.co.za
cometcartonline@gmail.com

---

## After they say yes

1. Ask them to fill in the form at https://www.cometcart.co.za/list-your-shop, or add them yourself from their reply.
2. In **Manage shops**: **Approve** them. If their reply allows photos, click **They agreed in writing**, and keep the email.
3. Shopify shops: click **Import from Shopify**. Others: import their spreadsheet.
4. Re-run the import every week or two to keep prices fresh.

## Tips

- **Smaller and mid-size South African online shops** are the most likely to say yes; big chains rarely reply until a site has lots of visitors.
- To tell if a shop uses Shopify: its checkout page usually mentions Shopify, or its web address ends in `.myshopify.com` behind the scenes. If you're unsure, ask them in your email.
- Keep each email personal (use their name and something you like about their shop). Under POPIA, an unsolicited message to a business's general address, inviting a free listing, is reasonable. Stop if they say no, and don't add them to any mailing list.
