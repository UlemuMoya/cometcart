# Starter products for Discover

Suggested first 16 real products: 2 per category where the same branded product is sold at several South African shops, so comparisons are useful.

These are suggestions only. **Check each one yourself before adding it.** Prices and stock change, and not every shop sells every item.

## How to add one (about 2 minutes)

1. Click a shop link below. It opens that shop's own search. Open the product and copy its link from the address bar.
2. On Cometcart, go to **Paste a link**, paste the link, type the price you see, and check the name and category.
3. Tick **Put it in Discover** (owner only), leave the photo empty, and click **Add to Discover**.
4. On the new product page, click **+ Add another shop's price**. Paste the same product's link from a second shop, type its price, and click **Add this price**.

Two rules:

- **Same product at every shop.** Match the model number, size or volume exactly, for example NA130/00, 125ml or 340g. If a shop has a different size, don't add it as the same product.
- **No photo for now.** A stock photo of headphones isn't *these* headphones. The card shows a clean category tile instead, until shop feeds provide real, licensed photos.
